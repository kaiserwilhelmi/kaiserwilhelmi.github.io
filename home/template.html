Forecast Method Comparison

Up to 14 methods · any of the 100 Market Watch series, or your own file

Give it a history and each method has to forecast months it hasn't seen, using nothing but the series' own past. Then it ranks them by how far off they were. The winner changes from one series to the next, so I don't pick a favorite ahead of time. I checked the numbers against a separate Python version.

Open Forecast Comparison
Data
Market Watch series, or a CSV or Excel file you upload
Tested on the last 24 months, 6 months ahead

Ranked by error, lower is better. The forecast is the average of the top three.

Industry Market Watch

17 industries · 80 cost series

Every industry has a page for the costs behind its prices, like materials, wages, fuel, freight and exchange rates. Each cost gets a 6-month forecast, a rating of Alert, Watch or Opportunity, and a short note on what a buyer could do about it.

Supplier Scorecard

Sample data, or upload your own purchase orders

I made up 12 office-supply suppliers and about 2,000 purchase orders, then scored each one for on-time delivery, fill rate, defects, invoice accuracy and price compliance. The chart shows which low scorers are getting the most money.

Side project

Loot Ledger

Search any of the 5,111 items in the online game Tibia and it lists every NPC that buys it, highest price first, with a map to each one. Paste your loot after a hunt and it tells you who to sell each item to.

Item and map data come from TibiaWiki (CC BY-SA 3.0) and TibiaMaps.io.
