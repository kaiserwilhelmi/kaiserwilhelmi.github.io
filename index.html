Which suppliers deserve more of our spend, and which need a corrective plan?

Weighted scorecard across 12 suppliers and ~2,000 purchase orders: on-time delivery, fill rate, defects, invoice accuracy and price compliance. Flags high-spend, low-performing suppliers and tracks trends; upload your own data to score real vendors.

Industry Market Watch

When should we lock in pricing, and when should we push suppliers for decreases?

Input-cost pressure for 17 industries, from aerospace to restaurants, built on 80 published price, wage, freight and currency series (BLS, EIA, IMF, Federal Reserve and Cass), plus EIA, USDA, Census import and USAspending data pulled directly from each agency. Each driver gets a 6-month forecast with a backtested 80% range and an Alert, Watch or Opportunity signal with a recommended buyer action. Updates daily.

Forecast Method Comparison

Which forecasting method should a buyer trust for this series, and how wrong is it likely to be?

Backtests up to 11 methods (naive benchmarks, exponential smoothing, Holt-Winters, seasonal ARIMA, regression and forecast combinations) month by month on freight, packaging, copper and jet fuel data, or on your own CSV. Scores each on MAE, MSE, RMSE, MAPE, sMAPE, MASE and bias, lets the data pick the winner, and checks whether that pick holds up across backtest windows. Every number is verified against an independent Python implementation.
